CODEX USAGE MINI - QUICK START

1. Keep CodexUsageMini.exe and CodexUsageMini.exe.config together.
2. Install Codex for Windows and sign in with your ChatGPT account.
3. Double-click CodexUsageMini.exe. No API key or installer is needed.

Colors use allowance remaining:
  Green: more than 50%
  Orange: 10% to 50%, inclusive
  Red: less than 10%
  Gray: unavailable or stale reading

Drag the heading to move. Pin toggles always on top.
Minus or Esc hides to tray. Double-click the tray icon to restore.
Refresh or F5 updates usage. X quits.
Right-click the widget or tray icon to preview all three colors.
Refresh exits preview mode and returns to live data.

Updates automatically every 60 seconds. Multiple reported limits are
supported; the lowest remaining allowance is the large number.
The app shows allowance percentages and reset times, not tokens left.

Requires Windows 10/11, .NET Framework 4.8, and native codex.exe.
To choose codex.exe explicitly, set CODEX_USAGE_MINI_CODEX to its full path.
