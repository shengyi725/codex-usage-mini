CODEX USAGE MINI v0.2.0 - WINDOWS PORTABLE

Keep CodexUsageMini.exe and CodexUsageMini.exe.config together.
Install Codex for Windows, sign in with ChatGPT, and open the EXE.
Requires Windows 10/11 and .NET Framework 4.8. No API key is needed.

ALLOWANCE
Green: more than 50% left. Orange: 10-50%, inclusive. Red: below 10%.
Gray: unavailable or stale. Refreshes every 60 seconds.

HOURLY AND MODEL HISTORY
Click the history icon or choose Hourly usage and models from the menu.
Hours shows activity and tokens by hour. Models compares recorded input,
cached input, output, totals, and event counts. Choose 24h, 7d, 30d, or 90d.
Statistics are imported from local Codex logs and retained for 90 days.
Export CSV saves hourly model totals. Other devices are not included.
Token totals are not the same as model-specific subscription quota charges.

APPEARANCE AND PLAN
Click the gear or choose Appearance and plan in the widget/tray menu.
The full widget starts at its original readable size. Click Percentage only
beside the gear for the small view. Hover and click its expand arrow to return.
Optional full-view scaling is 50-125%; compact text stays readable.
Transparency: 0-50%. Themes: Daylight, Dark, Ocean, Forest.
Subscription detection reads the paid-through date from Codex local sign-in
metadata automatically. Check now rereads it; Codex refreshes the source.
The source check time is shown in Settings; old readings say last known.
Paid through is a subscription-period end; renewal status may be unknown.
A manual expiry remains as a fallback and is never overwritten.
Automatic detection can be switched off in Settings. It requires readable
file-based ChatGPT sign-in; secure-store-only sign-ins may not expose dates.
No credential is sent or saved. Personal dates and history stay local.

CONTROLS
Click Percentage only beside the gear for the small percentage-only view.
Hover over the small window and click the expand arrow to restore it.
Clicking the percentage or pressing Ctrl+M also switches views.
Drag the heading, or drag the compact percentage, to move the widget.
Hover over the compact window (or use Tab) to reveal its buttons.
History and appearance open separately only when requested; right-click
also opens the menu. Pin toggles always on top.
Minus/Esc hides to tray; double-click the tray icon to restore.
Refresh/F5 refreshes allowance and exits color preview. X quits.
The tray menu remains full size even when the widget is small.

Set CODEX_USAGE_MINI_CODEX to an explicit codex.exe path if needed.
Set CODEX_USAGE_MINI_DATA to choose the private data folder.
